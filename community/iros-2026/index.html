Forceps-Based Fine Manipulation Challenge

Bringing people together around precise robotic manipulation.

IROS 2026Pittsburgh, USACompetition organizer

Hands-on with surgical robotic instruments

I helped organize a hands-on challenge that brought people together to explore the precision of teleoperated robotic instruments, through needle threading and M2 bolt pick-and-place.

Our goal was to introduce more people to surgical robotic platforms and the precision and control involved in manipulating small objects. Participants had the opportunity to experience the instruments through two deceptively simple tasks.

Task 01

Needle threading

Guide a thread through needle eyes using a teleoperated robotic instrument.

Task 02

M2 bolt pick-and-place

Pick up and place M2 bolts using a teleoperated robotic instrument.

A shared effort

I was grateful to help organize the challenge alongside Lueder A. Kahrs, Peter Kazanzides, Adnan Munawar, Loris Fichera, Thomas Looi, Tamás Haidegger, Christos Bergeles, Aban Merali, and Michael Yip.

Thank you to the participating platform teams from Johns Hopkins University’s LCSR, the University of Toronto’s MEDCVR Lab, and King’s College London’s RViM Lab, and to everyone who participated and supported the event.
